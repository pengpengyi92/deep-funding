# Funding Categories

Nine browse groups contain sixteen category tags. Each entity has one stable slug; multiple categories do not create duplicate institutions or scores.

| Group        | Category tags                                    | Primary review questions                                                           |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Incubators   | incubator                                        | Formation support, resources, costs, investment optionality                        |
| Accelerators | accelerator                                      | Program format, stage flexibility, participation requirements, capital optionality |
| Angel        | angel                                            | Authority, personal/syndicate mandate, stage and instrument                        |
| VC           | venture_capital, corporate_vc                    | Mandate from pre-seed to growth, ownership, follow-on capacity                     |
| PE           | private_equity                                   | Growth equity vs buyout vs special situations; financial underwriting              |
| Strategic    | strategic_investor                               | Commercial fit, conflicts, exclusivity and strategic rights                        |
| Government   | government_fund, industrial_fund, grant, subsidy | Jurisdiction, eligible entity, program objectives, restrictions                    |
| Banks & debt | bank, venture_debt                               | Repayment source, cash flow, covenants, collateral, debt capacity                  |
| Other        | family_office, crowdfunding, university_fund     | Specific mandate, platform rules, affiliation and conflicts                        |

Capital forms are separately equity, SAFE, convertible note, loan, credit line, venture debt, grant or subsidy. No form is inferred from a category. Venture debt can be supplied outside banks. Family offices may pursue several strategies.

A provider serving multiple offers chooses an explicit policy; never choose whichever policy gives the highest score. Distinct debt/equity programs need separate offer profiles until parent/offer modeling is introduced. The runtime refuses debt instruments under a non-credit policy.
