# Capital + Resource Taxonomy

Version: capital-resources-0.2.0. Updated: 2026-09-05.

Funding is not only money. Funding is the set of capital and resources that helps a founder or company reach its next stage.

Institution categories, investment instruments, operating maturity, financing history and non-financial resources are separate axes. Incubators are not angels; accelerators are not growth VC. A program may serve overlapping stages and may invest or provide resources only.
