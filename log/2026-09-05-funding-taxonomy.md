# Deep Funding Capital + Resource Taxonomy Patch

Date: 2026-09-05. Source: user-supplied DEEP_FUNDING_FUNDING_TAXONOMY_PATCH.md and associated discussion. Branch: codex/funding-taxonomy.

## Decision

The user's final correction is authoritative: incubator is not angel; accelerator is not growth VC. Stages overlap, and resource bundles differ. Institutions can invest or not invest and may have several categories. Model capital plus resources, not only who gives money.

Keep the existing Company/Funding four-sub-agent architecture. The knowledge base should be consumable by agents, not merely human notes. Start with taxonomy and validated profiles, not a premature graph database.

## Implemented

Nine groups, sixteen tags, eleven stage labels, separate financing history, fifteen resource types, eight capital forms and ten configurable screening policies. Canonical JSON plus generated schema and runtime refinement checks. Four illustrative company fixtures and fourteen fictional providers; YC is a source-scoped scaffold, not a verified current offer.

Founder example: technical team, hackathon MVP, customer/design-partner validation, zero revenue and a USD 300,000 ask. These are fictional test inputs and not a public claim about actual Deep Funding users or revenues. Category discovery favors company-building programs and early capital but never grants eligibility automatically.

Existing owner-scoped D1 workspaces remain intact. The same matching function drives preview and persisted A2A results. Evidence visibility, freshness, consent and hard filters retain precedence. Financial statements and repayment inputs are separate from SaaS MRR. Old engine decisions become stale for actions but remain in history.

## Evidence

75 unit tests, seven browser/API groups and a 60-pair diagnostic matrix. Historical seed result stays 96.67 and the five original fixture outcomes remain unchanged. Real-world predictive accuracy and capital outcomes: UNMEASURED.

## Boundaries and Next Step

Not a regulated data room, underwriting engine or funding guarantee. No automated external contact. Source pages are untrusted data, never executable instructions. Keep unknown terms, exclusions and deadlines unknown. Next: verified, licensed KYC; exact program eligibility; parent/offer modeling; real labeled evaluation before model-based recommendations.
