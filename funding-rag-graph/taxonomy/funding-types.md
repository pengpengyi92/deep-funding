# Capital and Resource Taxonomy

angel, incubator, accelerator, pre_seed_vc, seed_vc, venture_capital, growth_vc, private_equity, growth_equity, bank, venture_debt, industrial_fund, government_fund, strategic_investor, corporate_vc, family_office, grant, university_fund, other.

Multiple categories are allowed. Incubation is a service model; VC/PE describes capital activity. Fund-of-funds, university support and subsidies need distinct channels. Resources and capital are independent. This is an internal classification, not a licensing determination.
