# Company Profiles

Real company records remain in private, expiring workspaces, not this public repository. Four fictional test profiles live in `data/taxonomy-demo.ts`: idea founder, two-founder pre-seed AI MVP, revenue-generating growth company and mature bootstrapped company.

The MVP has a reported product, five customer/design-partner validations, zero MRR and a USD 300,000 single-provider request. These are illustrative inputs, not claims about Pengyi or Deep Funding's actual customers or financing.

CompanyStage and financingRound are distinct. Financial disclosures use nullable annual revenue, operating cash flow, debt, annual debt service, repayment source and statement availability. Current shared financials evidence is required for scoring. The data room, independently audited accounts, EBITDA/valuation models, collateral/covenant underwriting and syndicate allocations are not implemented.

Do not convert missing numbers into zero. Do not equate design partners with paying customers. Readiness measures evidence coverage, not company quality or probability of funding.
